# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and evidence focused. Critique ideas and code, not people. Welcome
contributors regardless of experience, identity, background, or language, and give credit when
someone's work or idea materially shapes a contribution.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption
are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, release discussions,
and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer profile or GitHub's available private
reporting channels. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
